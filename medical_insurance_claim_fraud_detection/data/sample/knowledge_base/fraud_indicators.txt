Fraud Indicators
- Unusually high claim amount vs peer group (>3 std dev).
- Upcoding: billing for more expensive procedure than performed.
- Duplicate billing same date same procedure.
- Patient age inconsistent with diagnosis.
- Provider billing far outside specialty (e.g., orthopedics billing cardiology).
- Claim submission date before service date.
- Inconsistent provider location vs patient location without referral.
- Missing discharge summary for inpatient claims.
- Altered documents, mismatched totals, overlapping dates.
- High frequency claims same patient in short window.
- Provider with history of high fraud rate.
- Unbundling: splitting procedure into separate billable components.
