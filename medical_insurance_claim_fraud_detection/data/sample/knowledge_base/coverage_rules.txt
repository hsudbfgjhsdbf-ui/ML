Coverage Rules
- Inpatient coverage includes room, surgery, anesthesia up to policy limit.
- Outpatient: consultations and labs covered at 80% after deductible.
- Emergency: 100% covered if genuine emergency, else 70%.
- Deductible $500 per year, out-of-pocket max $5000.
- Pre-authorization: required for MRI, CT, major surgery.
- Generic prescriptions preferred; branded need justification.
