## 10.1 Grounding in Indian Healthcare Realities
Unlike Western datasets (such as US Medicare/Medicaid), the Indian health insurance landscape features unique structural, legal, and operational nuances:

1. **Healthcare Provider Tiers & Dynamic Pricing:**
   - *Tier 1 Metro Super-Specialty:* Hospitals such as Apollo, Fortis, and Manipal with comprehensive ICUs and advanced robotic surgery (high standard cost).
   - *Tier 2 City Multispecialty:* Centers such as SDM College of Medical Sciences Dharwad and KIMS Hubballi (moderate benchmark cost).
   - *Tier 3 Community & Nursing Homes:* Unaccredited private nursing homes in Tier 2/3 districts (lower standard cost, higher billing fraud risk).

2. **Policy Structures:**
   - *Family Floater Plans:* Shared sum insured among spouses, children, and parents.
   - *Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY):* Government-sponsored cashless coverage for secondary and tertiary care.
   - *Senior Citizen Red Carpet Policies:* Specific co-payment schedules (10%–20%) and shorter waiting periods.

3. **IRDAI Statutory Compliance:**
   - Enforces strict 30-day claim settlement timelines.
   - Mandates that any claim denial must cite specific policy exclusion clauses in written form.
   - Requires bilingual communication and clear grievance redressal mechanisms for policyholders.
